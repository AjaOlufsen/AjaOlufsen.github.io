About

Redesigning a manual workflow before automating it.

Barnabókaklubbin is an Icelandic children's book club with administrative workflows involving recurring manual handling of member and order data.

The project explored how these processes could be analysed, redesigned and partially automated through robotic process automation.

The challenge

Understanding where the workflow was creating friction.

We mapped the existing process step by step to identify repetitive tasks, bottlenecks and points where information had to be handled manually.

Rather than immediately automating individual tasks, we approached the workflow as a complete system.

The solution

Turning process analysis into an automated workflow.

Based on the process analysis, we redesigned parts of the workflow and developed an RPA solution using Microsoft Power Automate.

The aim was to reduce repetitive administrative work while preserving the parts of the process where human input was still necessary.

Automation was not the starting point.
Understanding the process was.

My role

I worked with process analysis, workflow design and the development of the automation solution.

My role involved translating the existing administrative process into a structured digital workflow that could be automated reliably.

Process

The project moved from process mapping and analysis into workflow redesign and technical prototyping.

This made it possible to identify which tasks were suitable for automation and which points still required human judgement or intervention.

Design approach

Treating automation as a design problem, not just a technical one.

The project focused on how automation could support the organisation without simply reproducing inefficient manual processes in digital form.

By analysing the workflow first, the automation could be designed around the actual structure of the work rather than around isolated tasks.
