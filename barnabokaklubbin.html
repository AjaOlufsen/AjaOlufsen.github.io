BARNABÓKAKLUBBIN

Digital Transformation · Process Automation · 2025

Automating a manual membership workflow

Barnabókaklubbin is an Icelandic children's book club. The project explored how a repetitive and time-consuming administrative workflow could be redesigned and automated.

We mapped the existing process, identified bottlenecks and manual tasks, and developed an RPA solution using Microsoft Power Automate. The goal was not simply to automate individual tasks, but to rethink the workflow as a whole and create a more efficient digital process.
