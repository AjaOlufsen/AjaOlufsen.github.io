Barnabókaklubbin

Digital Transformation · Process Optimisation · RPA · 2025

About

Barnabókaklubbin is an Icelandic children's book club with administrative workflows involving recurring manual handling of member and order data.

The project explored how these existing processes could be analysed, redesigned and partially automated through robotic process automation.

The Challenge

We mapped the existing workflow step by step to identify repetitive tasks, bottlenecks and points where information had to be handled manually.

Rather than immediately automating individual tasks, we approached the workflow as a complete system and examined how information moved through the organisation.

Automation was not the starting point.
Understanding the process was.

The Solution

Based on the process analysis, we redesigned parts of the workflow and developed an RPA solution using Microsoft Power Automate.

The aim was to reduce repetitive administrative work while preserving the parts of the process where human input was still necessary.

My Role

I worked with process analysis, workflow design and the development of the automation solution, translating the existing administrative process into a structured digital workflow.
