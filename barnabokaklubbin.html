BARNABÓKAKLUBBIN

Process Automation · Digital Transformation · RPA · 2025

THE CHALLENGE

What happens when a growing digital service still depends on repetitive manual work behind the scenes?

Barnabókaklubbin is an Icelandic children's book club with administrative processes that required recurring manual handling of member and order data. Our task was to investigate the existing workflow and identify where automation could reduce repetitive work and create a more efficient process.

PROCESS

From manual workflow to automated process

We began by mapping the existing workflow step by step. Rather than automating individual actions immediately, we looked at the process as a system: where information entered the workflow, how it moved between tasks, and where unnecessary manual work occurred.

Based on this analysis, we redesigned parts of the workflow and developed an RPA solution using Microsoft Power Automate.

APPROACH

Automation was not the starting point.
Understanding the process was.

SOLUTION

Designing the automation

The resulting solution automated repetitive steps within the existing administrative process while preserving the points where human input was still needed.

The project became an exercise in process optimisation as much as automation: translating organisational routines into a structured digital workflow that could be executed consistently.
