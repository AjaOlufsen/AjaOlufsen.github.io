Jellyfish

Interactive Light Installation · Roskilde Festival · 2025

About

Jellyfish is an interactive light installation created for Roskilde Festival 2025. The installation was designed as a serene pause within the intensity of the festival environment.

Visitors were invited to slow down and experience calm through light, tactile materials and subtle sensory interaction.

The Concept

We explored how light and embodied interaction could create a momentary escape from the noise and energy of the festival.

Inspired by the calm, fluid movement of jellyfish, the installation invited visitors to engage with its soft materials, gentle movement and changing light. The intention was to create a meditative sensory experience rather than demand attention.

From Sketch to Installation

Through iterative prototyping and hands-on material exploration, we developed the installation using yarn, bubble wrap, tulle, NeoPixel LEDs and chicken wire.

The materials were chosen for their softness, transparency and ability to diffuse light, helping create the flowing and organic expression of the final installation.

Experience

The final installation created a small sensory space within the festival environment where visitors could pause, breathe and engage with the work at their own pace.
