Jellyfish

Interactive Light Installation · Roskilde Festival · 2025

A sensory light installation designed to create a quiet, meditative pause within the intensity of Roskilde Festival.

Embodied Interaction Physical Computing Material Exploration Prototyping
About

Designing a moment of calm in the middle of festival chaos.

Jellyfish is an interactive light installation created for Roskilde Festival 2025.

The installation invited visitors to slow down and engage with gentle light, tactile materials and subtle sensory interaction in contrast to the surrounding energy of the festival.

The concept

Using light and embodied interaction to create a temporary escape.

We explored how an installation could offer festival visitors a momentary break from noise, movement and social intensity.

Inspired by the calm and fluid qualities of jellyfish, the installation combined changing light, soft tactile materials and gentle movement.

Rather than demanding attention, the installation was designed to invite slower and more reflective interaction.

From sketch to installation

Building the atmosphere through material exploration.

The installation developed through iterative prototyping and hands-on experimentation with form, light and material.

We worked with yarn, bubble wrap, tulle, chicken wire and NeoPixel LEDs to create a soft, translucent structure that could diffuse light and produce an organic visual expression.

The material qualities became an important part of the interaction, shaping both how the installation looked and how visitors physically approached it.

Experience

Creating space for visitors to pause, breathe and engage at their own pace.

The final installation created a small sensory environment within the larger festival space.

Visitors could approach, touch and observe the installation without being given a specific task or goal, allowing the interaction to remain open-ended and personal.
