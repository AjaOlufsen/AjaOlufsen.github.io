The Contraception Line

Interactive Installation · Roskilde Festival · 2026

An interactive telephone installation exploring contraception, responsibility and sexual health through anonymous participation at Roskilde Festival.

Interaction Design Physical Computing Arduino Prototyping
About

A playful and anonymous way to start conversations about contraception.

The Contraception Line was developed for Roskilde Festival as an interactive installation exploring contraception, sexual health and responsibility.

Rather than presenting visitors with static information, the installation invited them to participate and contribute their own anonymous responses.

The interaction

Turning a familiar object into an anonymous conversation.

Visitors interact with the installation through a telephone receiver and physical buttons.

A short series of questions asks about sexual activity, contraception and responsibility. The responses are collected anonymously and used to visualise patterns in festival behaviour.

My role

I worked on the concept, interaction design, physical prototyping and technical development of the installation, including the interaction flow and the physical experience.

Process

The project developed through iterative concept development, physical prototyping, programming and testing, with a strong focus on making the interaction simple and intuitive.

Prototyping

Building a robust interaction for a busy festival environment.

The physical setup had to withstand repeated use while remaining easy to understand without lengthy instructions.

The familiar telephone receiver created a clear invitation to interact, while physical buttons kept the experience direct and accessible.
