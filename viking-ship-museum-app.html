Viking Ship Museum App

Interaction Design · Digital Museum Experience · Prototyping · 2024

A playful mobile museum experience combining QR-code interaction, exploration and historical storytelling through an interactive treasure hunt.

Interaction Design Design Sprint 2.0 p5.js User Testing Digital Prototyping

Context

The project was developed around a case from the Viking Ship Museum in Roskilde, which wanted to explore new ways of communicating activities, information and historical content through digital technology.

We focused on how a mobile experience could make the museum visit more active and engaging by encouraging visitors to explore physical locations rather than only receive information passively.

The challenge

Existing information displays could be difficult for international visitors to interpret, while some parts of the museum needed alternative ways of communicating historical knowledge when staff were not present.

The design challenge therefore became how to connect historical learning with exploration, movement and playful interaction.

How can learning feel like exploration rather than instruction?

The concept

The concept evolved into a mobile treasure hunt aimed primarily at younger visitors.

Guests could scan QR codes positioned throughout the museum, meet animated Viking characters and complete missions to progress through the experience.

Physical + digital interaction

The app was not designed as a standalone screen experience.

QR codes linked the interface to physical museum locations, making movement through the exhibition part of the interaction itself.

Design process

The project developed through several iterations, beginning with speculative design and the question: What if Vikings still existed in contemporary society?

This exploration gradually moved towards a more concrete museum experience through ideation, storyboarding and Design Sprint 2.0.

Prototype development

A functional prototype was developed with particular focus on the QR-code interaction.

This became the central connection between the museum's physical environment and the digital experience.

User testing

The prototype was tested with users and museum staff to evaluate both the interface and the overall visitor experience.

Participants responded positively to the interactive treasure hunt, particularly the animated Viking characters and the QR-code interaction.

Design refinement

Testing helped us refine the relationship between entertainment and historical learning.

The aim was to make information feel integrated into the activity rather than something added on top of the interaction.
